Column names equal the ontology property local names. Follow `container_manager_mcp/ontology/access_contracts.ttl`. Save one recorded response per entity in `tests/recordings/<entity>.json`. Medulla copies these into its competency fixtures. The consumer is the medulla table row MEDULLA-CQ-CONN-R086. | Test: `tests/test_access_contracts.py::test_competency_contracts_certified` (L2) runs the connector ontology certification and validates each recording against its contract. |

The consumer is MEDULLA-SUPPLY-VOCAB-R006. | Test: `tests/test_access_contracts.py::test_pod_container_image_contracts` (L2) runs the ontology certification for the three rows. |
